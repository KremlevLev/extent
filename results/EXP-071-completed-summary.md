## Depth behavior

From depth 8 to depth 12, `TEACHER` NLL jumps by `+1.776001/+2.062225`. `ONPOLICY` changes by only `-0.019162/+0.077146`; `MIXED` changes by `+0.050989/+0.129987`. Thus sequential deployment-distribution recovery does not merely shift all curves by a constant: it specifically prevents the late collapse observed when each new block continues training only on clean Qwen residuals.

At depth 1, `TEACHER` and `ONPOLICY` are byte-identical because no upstream replacement exists. Small `MIXED` differences there arise from a different deterministic ordering of duplicated identical examples and are not mechanism evidence. At depths 4 and 8, `ONPOLICY` already beats `TEACHER` in both seeds. `MIXED` is near-neutral for seed 456 until the depth-12 collapse, so the strict final/AUC gate rather than a claim of uniform milestone superiority is the valid registered result.

## Interpretation

EXP-070's input-shift observation is now actionable: conditioning each replacement on the residual stream generated by its actual upstream hybrid substantially improves composed language-model NLL. Pure on-policy recovery is the best observed arm, but it was secondary in EXP-071; selecting it as the primary method requires a fresh-data locked confirmation.

Quality is not recovered to Qwen (`teacher NLL 3.315843`), and only the first 12 of 24 Mamba positions were converted. The result does not cover all-Mamba depth, new model seeds, MLA, 14B scaling, long context or throughput.

## Decision

Advance to EXP-072: repeat the comparison from the original matched endpoints on fresh text, increase recovery to 4,096 updates at context 128, evaluate 32 held-out windows, and continue through all 24 Mamba positions. Pre-register `ONPOLICY` as primary based on EXP-071; retain `MIXED` and `TEACHER` as controls. Do not alter EXP-071 or reinterpret `ONPOLICY` as its original primary endpoint.
